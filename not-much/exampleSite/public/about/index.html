🦊 Who is Eriol Fox?

My Career story

For the more narratively inclined, I discovered a love for Free and Libre Open Source Software (FLOSS, FOSS or OSS) after being introduced to the concept a couple of times in my early career and then found my place in OSS when i joined Ushahidi and was welcomed into communities like Mozilla’s Open Leaders, Sustain OSS and Open Source Design. I started contributing as a Designer to OSS projects actiely and advocating for design as an essential part of the critical infrastructural sustainability of OSS with the hypothesis being:

“If your open source software isn’t usable and understandable by average, everyday people then adoption of OSS and the power to collaborate on OSS will continue to consolidate with people that have the time and privilage to learn and use technical skills”

OSS should be accessible and usable to anyone who wants to start with or move over to open source, espeically when it’s foundational to both the free and open internet and the future as it pertains to soverign technology for populations.
