#### Eriol speaks on Design in Open Source, Design that prioritises Privacy and Security and Trauma informed, Inclusive design from a Digital Human Rights background.
<br>

Eriol has been speaking across the UK and internationally since 2016.

[Email](mailto:erioldoesdesign@gmail.com) or DM me on social media about speaking/lecturing/workshop opportunities at your event. 

Eriol is available for speaking/lecturering and workshop facilitation both paid and unpaid. They prefer to have travel and accomodation covered but can also seek funds to support travel and accomodation.

You can view recordings of some of Eriol's talks and workshops at various conferences on a [Youtube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLwz4EueITgvmJzrNWbGkAMeDVLlOWQuch)
